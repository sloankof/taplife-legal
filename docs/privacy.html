Privacy Policy

App: TapLife: History Life Sim
Publisher: Kofford Industries LLC
Effective date: November 16, 2026

TapLife is designed to run on your device. You do not need an account to play. In the current version, TapLife does not upload your game save or personal information to Kofford Industries servers, does not show third-party ads, and does not process real-money payments.

This page itself does not use cookies, analytics, or advertising.

Information stored on your device

To keep your progress, settings, and unlocked items between sessions, TapLife stores data in the app’s local storage and a local offline cache on your device. This can include:

  • Your game progress and settings (for example, difficulty, accessibility options, sound, and a reminder preference flag).
  • Character appearance, wardrobe, collectibles, achievements, run history, and in-game currency.
  • Small local flags, such as whether you have finished character setup or already seen the opening screen.
  • A short local log of in-app gameplay events, limited to the most recent 200 entries (for example, difficulty or tap-style choices). In the current version, this log stays on your device and is not sent to Kofford Industries or to any third party.
  • A local cache of game files so the app can work offline.

TapLife does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, camera, microphone, or location. There is no sign-in and no user profile on our servers.

Advertising

Third-party advertising is turned off in the current version. The app does not access an advertising identifier (such as IDFA) and does not include a live advertising SDK.

Optional rewarded ads may be added in a future update (for example, through an ad mediation partner). If that happens, we will update this Privacy Policy and the App Store privacy details before that version is released.

In-app purchases

The current version includes a practice shop and a Restore Purchases control for later store setup. TapLife does not process payment cards, Apple billing, or transaction receipts in this version, and no real-money purchase is completed in the app today.

If Apple In-App Purchases are turned on in a later release, purchases will be processed by Apple under Apple’s terms and privacy policy. We will update this Privacy Policy before that version ships.

Notifications

Push notifications are not active in the current version. A “Remind me tomorrow” setting may appear as unavailable until a future update enables notifications. If notifications are added, we will update this policy first.

Network use

The app may load its own game files, art, and audio from the app package or from the local cache on your device. The current version does not send your save data or the local gameplay log to our servers.

Clipboard

If you choose to share a finished run or a similar summary, the app may copy a plain-text summary (for example, an ending title and stats) to your device clipboard. This happens only when you take that action.

Children’s privacy

TapLife is intended for a general audience aged 12 and older. It is not in Apple’s Kids Category and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or from other players, because the current version keeps play data on the device and does not create accounts.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us at sloan@koffordindustries.com. We will review the request and delete any personal information we hold.

Deleting your data

You can remove locally stored TapLife data at any time:

  • In the app: open Settings and choose Delete / Reset Saved Data. After you confirm, the app permanently deletes your local save and local analytics data on that device. This cannot be undone.
  • On the device: delete the app, or clear the app or site storage. That also removes locally stored TapLife data.
  • By email: write to sloan@koffordindustries.com if you need help with a deletion request. Because the current version stores play data only on your device, we usually cannot erase a local save remotely. We will help you use the in-app control or delete the app.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app’s data practices change, including if advertising, Apple In-App Purchases, crash reporting, or push notifications are added. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page. Please check this page for the latest version.
